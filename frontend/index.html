MilestoneProof

Freelance milestones, accepted by validator consensus — not by argument.

How it works

  1. A client creates a milestone with written acceptance criteria.
  2. The worker submits evidence that the work is done.
  3. Anyone runs the validator assessment: independent GenLayer validators judge the evidence against the criteria and must agree exactly on the verdict and reason category.
  4. ACCEPTED and REJECTED are terminal. NEEDS_WORK lets the worker submit fixed evidence and be re-assessed.

The verdict judges the submitted evidence against the submitted criteria only. It does not verify links, run code, or move funds.
